Stop asking "are you sure?" You only get its answer twice.
The real check is habit five. Four come first.
One. Narrow the question.
"Give me a business idea" gets an idea that fits anyone.
Ask for a shortlist. Add your limits, like ten hours a week.
Then ask which one to start with.
Two. One action per step.
Give it two jobs in one step, and it guesses at one.
So split it.
Three. Pick the tool for the job, not the tab that's open.
Four. Chain them. One tool does the work, another checks it.
Five. Back to "are you sure?"
Ask Claude or Gemini the exact same question.
Then check it against a named source.
Sounding sure doesn't count.
