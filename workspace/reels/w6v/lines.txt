Before AI touches your resume, ask for 3 lists.
Skip that, and "ran the café's Instagram" turns into "spearheaded a cross-channel brand strategy."
Sounds nothing like you. And you'll have to explain it in the interview.
So paste two things. The job posting, in full. Then your resume.
Now ask for 3 lists.
One: which of your bullet points this job actually wants.
Two: which ones are dead weight here. Like that barista job from 2022.
Three: what the posting asks for that you never mention. Say, Canva.
Now you're editing what you really did. Not an inflated version.
The third list is where the work is. Done it? Add it in your own words. Haven't? Leave it out.
