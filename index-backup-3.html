Same question. Different method.

Three situations, taken from the guide.

01

Finding an idea

Generic request
Give me a business idea.
Result: an answer that could belong to anyone.
With the method, three prompts, each built on the last
1
Research realistic ways to [your goal]. Give me a shortlist, with a short explanation of each.
2
Research each of these in more depth, and tell me which two or three are the most realistic for someone with [your real constraint].
3
Of these, which one specifically should I start with, and why, based on everything you just found?
Broad on purposeThen narrowerDown to one answer
02

Building something

Generic request
Make me a Pinterest pin that will sell this product.
Result: guesses about the platform, and a look anyone could spot as AI.
With the method, separate prompts for separate jobs
1
Research the Pinterest algorithm and SEO, and build the most effective design approach based on that research.
2
Research what makes a design look obviously AI-generated, and identify how to avoid those issues in this one.
3
Turn this into a step-by-step guide to build it. Every step must contain exactly one action. If a step would require doing two things, split it into two separate steps.
One job per promptResearch firstOne action per step
03

Checking an answer

Generic request
Are you sure?
Result: the same tool confirms what it already said.
With the method, a three-step check
1
The trigger. A real result doesn't match what you were told. That mismatch is the signal to check.
2
The check. Ask a different AI tool the exact same question, not the same tool again.
3
The confirmation. Test the answer against the real world or a named official source, not against which one sounds more convincing.
