What it fixes

The habits readers notice.

One habit means little. Five in one paragraph is what makes text read as AI-written. The instructions target the ones from Wikipedia's “Signs of AI writing” list that show up in ordinary writing.

Importance inflation

“marks a pivotal moment”, “a testament to”

Bans the phrases, asks for the plain fact

The contrast formula

“It's not just a tool, it's a way of working.”

Bans “not just X, but Y”

Formatting overload

Headings, bullets and bold on a three-line answer

Plain paragraphs unless you ask

Recaps and closers

“In conclusion…”, “Let me know if you'd like…”

No recaps, no closing offers

None of these habits proves a text was written by AI, and fixing them doesn't make weak writing strong. It removes the most obvious signals.
