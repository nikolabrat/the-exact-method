  flops; his real number ($150k/mo) and real data (21,346 screens) work.

### Patterns (new, on top of Batch 1)

1. **"⚠️ Warning:" + a list of 3** is cracked.artificial's main engine (4 of his top 7). The warning is ironic:
   the danger is that it's good / free / unfair.
2. **"Send this to a ___" as the caption's first line** sits on his 2 biggest (1.0M, 375K) and the hustler one.
   Highest comment counts of the whole batch (2-3.4K).
3. **Tier list ("I finally ranked them")** got a part two. A ranking makes people argue in the comments.
4. **Real data in → a copyable structure out** (ernesto 21,346 screens; cracked's paywall gallery). Proof by volume.
5. **"The best X is no X" contrarian simplicity** (rubenhassid) fits our user's own style.
6. **Exact-prompt keyword again** (rubenhassid GUIDE on the one-prompt websites), backing W-7.
