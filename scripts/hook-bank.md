### What this changes for us

1. **Result first is the rule, confirmed:** 15 of 22 show the finished result or the save-able page by 1.5 s.
   Our frame-0 test (2) stands.
2. **"Look at…" opens 4 of okaashish's top 5.** A command plus the result on screen, not "I" and not a question.
3. **Short save-able loops (H4) are the gap.** 4 outliers between 6 and 13 s. Fits the Short-Reel test. Candidates:
   idea 1 (3 levels of asking ChatGPT what to sell), idea 15 (tier list), the PLAIN never-list as a one-page
   cheat sheet.
4. **Don't copy cracked's black frame 0.** It works for him because 50 Reels built a recognisable persona and the
   spoken line carries it. We have 5 followers. Take his **words** (H5 warning), keep our full frame 0.
5. **Captions:** all 22 caption the speech, 1-3 words or one short line, centred low or mid. Matches our max-3-words rule.
6. **The weakest alignment was the longest Reel** (rubenhassid 145 s). Keep to 15-25 s, or 40-60 s with the §11 structure.
