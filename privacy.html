Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy describes how The Exact Method handles personal information when you visit this website or buy a digital product. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of The Exact Method.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include:

  • Contact and order details you provide, such as your name and email address, to receive a product or get purchase support.
  • Checkout and transaction details needed to process a purchase, such as the product, order status, and payment confirmation.
  • Browser storage used to remember your response to the notice on this website. The consent preference is stored in your browser's local storage.

Payment card credentials are handled by the checkout provider, not stored by this website. This website does not currently contain an on-page checkout form. If a third-party checkout or delivery provider is used, that provider may collect payment and delivery details directly and may share order information with us so we can fulfill your purchase.

How we use your information

We use relevant information to confirm and deliver digital purchases, provide requested purchase support, send product-related updates where appropriate, maintain transaction records, and protect the service from misuse. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We share only the information needed with service providers involved in checkout, payment, delivery, or support. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms. We do not use information collected through this website for third-party advertising.

Secure payment processing

When a purchase is offered, payment is processed by the checkout provider displayed to you. That provider is responsible for securing payment credentials and processing the payment. We receive only the transaction and contact details needed to confirm and deliver your order; this website does not store full payment card numbers or security codes.

No method of online transmission or storage is completely secure. We take reasonable steps to limit access to information we receive and to use it only for the purposes described in this policy.

Retention

We keep order and contact information for as long as reasonably needed to deliver products, provide support, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. The consent preference remains in your browser until you clear local storage or remove the saved entry.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to receive a portable copy. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing. We do not sell personal information.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection or privacy authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.
