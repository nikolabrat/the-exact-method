The method

Three prompts. One starting point.

Ask a one-line question and you get an answer that fits anyone. Run these three in order, one at a time, and every answer hands the next prompt something real to narrow.

1

Go broad, on purpose

"Research realistic ways to [your goal]. Give me a shortlist, with a short explanation of each."

Its only job is to produce real material. No final answer yet.

2

Narrow with your real limits

"Research each of these in more depth, and tell me which two or three are the most realistic for someone with [your real constraint: time, budget, experience, equipment, etc.]."

This is where you add what a stranger wouldn't know.

3

Narrow to one choice

"Of these, which one specifically should I start with, and why, based on everything you just found?"

You get one recommendation and the reason for it. You decide.
